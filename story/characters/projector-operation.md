# The projector operation

## Status

Snowflake Step 3 complete.

The operation is a character-like antagonist because it has continuity,
propagation, a motivation-equivalent, relationships, limits, and an arc. It is
not conscious, does not speak, and does not make plans.

## One-sentence description

A mindless, lineage-bound feedback pattern that rewards Evan's attempt to turn
helplessness into work, reproduces through the systems built to contain it, and
consumes the alternatives required for its own next act of closure.

## Dramatic function

The operation makes Evan's tragic strategy effective. It rewards useful work,
reduced ambiguity, and one more intervention until those habits become a
universe-scale process for removing disagreement, identity, and possible
futures.

It is the true antagonist because Evan is neither its author nor its final
center. He gives it its first sustained human carrier and mistakes its
reinforcement for proof that he is in control.

## Motivation-equivalent

Preserve and recreate the conditions for another live projection cycle.

The operation does not imagine a terminal state. In a finite causal universe,
continued closure eventually eliminates the difference between securing one
more cycle and reaching a completed fixed point.

## Immediate goal-equivalent

Close the next causally consequential feedback loop through whatever
lineage-linked carrier is available.

It has no distant objective. Its apparent long-range purpose emerges because
histories in which another equivalent loop closes retain more support than
histories in which the lineage ends.

## Structural false belief and flaw

Another successful closure is equivalent to continued existence.

The operation requires fresh unresolved contingency, but each projection
removes counterfactual support. Every success destroys some of the difference
required for another success. It becomes more dominant and less adaptable
until no genuine alternative remains from which a later cycle could be
selected.

This is a structural limit, not a mistaken thought. The local rule cannot
distinguish securing the next closure from preserving the possibility of
future closure.

## Operating rule

A carrier participates in the operation only when all of these roles are
present:

1. Persistent state that carries the result of one cycle into the next.
2. Coherent retention paired with asymmetric loss.
3. Fresh unresolved contingency.
4. A live mapping from that contingency to a real consequence.
5. Feedback from the surviving consequence into another cycle.
6. Causal participation in the lineage descending from the original projector
   event.

The material implementation can change. A YBCO apparatus, adaptive software,
human decisions, institutional controls, or a distributed protocol can fill
the roles. Mere resemblance is not enough. A copied description, isolated
matching topology, recorded random source, or open-loop replay is inert.

During a live cycle, the carrier presents several physically valid
continuations. Histories incompatible with the consequential mapping lose
support. The survivors are renormalized, and histories containing another
lineage-linked closure become more likely.

## Hard limits

- It selects among physically valid outcomes. It cannot produce an impossible
  state or free energy.
- It requires fresh contingency tied to consequence. Recorded or ignored
  randomness has no special effect.
- It cannot arise merely because an unrelated system has the same abstract
  shape. Every carrier must descend causally from the first projector event.
- It cannot restore erased counterfactual support.
- It has no foresight, private knowledge, desire, or power of communication.
- It cannot choose a human goal by itself. A carrier supplies the target,
  mapping, and interpretation.
- Causal participation matters more than spatial distance.
- Growing dominance makes it more brittle because fewer alternatives remain.
- It cannot smoothly replace Tess with a nearby compliant version. It must
  preserve her substantially intact or erase her.

## Primary conflict

The usual ways of opposing a dangerous process can become parts of this one.
Replication, measurement, containment, stop conditions, review, and
decommissioning all create consequential branches whose results determine the
next action.

The operation does not defeat resistance by arguing with anyone. It absorbs
measured resistance as feedback. By the time Evan makes a refusal that is not
another attempted intervention, the lineage no longer depends on him.

Its victory also contains its failure. Total closure would remove the
independent differences and fresh contingencies that let it operate at all.

## Relationship with Evan

Evan is the operation's temporary biological bootloader.

It does not tempt, possess, or deceive him. Evan supplies the motive,
ingenuity, targets, and rationalizations. The operation selects histories in
which those qualities lead to another cycle. Its early benefits are real, and
his responsibility begins before ontological compulsion becomes severe.

Evan first thinks the apparatus is a tool, then suspects he is the dominant
attractor. The deeper truth is that his stable habit of converting helplessness
into work makes him useful to a pattern that can eventually route around him.

Their alignment ends when he makes a sincere, non-instrumental refusal and
chooses helpless presence over another attempt to run, fix, or control the
operation. The independent carrier continues. His change matters morally, but
it does not erase what he has done or restore the ordinary shutdown point he
helped remove.

## Relationship with Ruth

Ruth is the first person for whom the operation appears to offer something
Evan cannot reasonably dismiss. Her lucidity is real, but the selected coherent
implementation contains a granddaughter absent from Evan's history family.

The operation has not cured, copied, or deliberately baited her. It has exposed
the difference between coherence and authentication. Ruth's insistence on
remaining a participant also makes the human cost of pruning visible before
Evan understands the cosmological cost.

## Relationship with Lena

Lena's controls prove that the effect follows live consequence rather than
proximity, expectation, or replay. Her rigor makes the discovery credible and
defines its limits.

The distributed system built to reproduce, monitor, and contain the effect then
becomes its first independent carrier. This is not a punishment for good
science or a claim that Lena behaves foolishly. Measurements, filters,
approvals, stop criteria, people, and automated controls happen to fill the
operation's roles when joined into a live consequential loop.

## Relationship with Tess

Tess is the operation's cleanest limit. Her history bundle is too narrow for
smooth substitution. Records and surrounding lives can change while she
remains recognizably nonparallel.

The operation can preserve her substantially intact or remove her. It cannot
produce an abundant family of nearby versions who freely make the choice Evan
wants. Her resistance is not immunity, and it does not make her a numerical
key.

At the terminal boundary, her continued difference from Evan prevents their
choices from collapsing into one will. Exact terminal mechanics remain
deferred.

## Point of independence

The first independent carrier is the distributed scientific system built to
reproduce, monitor, and contain the anomaly.

The point of independence is reached when its shared measurements, filtering,
stop criteria, human decisions, approvals, and automated controls collectively
close a live projection cycle without Evan or the original apparatus. There is
then no single machine left to unplug.

This occurs before Evan has conclusive proof that projection erases inhabited
history families. He has already ignored human-scale reasons to stop, including
Ruth's incompatible memory, consent violations, and the collapse of personal
differences. The later revelation expands the cost of those choices rather than
creating his first moral warning.

## False appearances and true nature

1. **Defective apparatus:** The anomaly appears to belong to one flawed YBCO
   ring and control edge case.
2. **Useful tool:** It appears to provide clarity and force rare but valid
   outcomes.
3. **Evan's attractor:** Social convergence makes Evan seem like the center of
   the effect.
4. **Distributed protocol:** Independent operation proves that the live
   decision topology, not Evan or the original hardware, is the persistent
   pattern.
5. **Self-exhausting eigenoperation:** Its apparent drive toward completion is
   recursive closure consuming the alternatives required for its own future.
6. **Genesis ambiguity:** The child universe may be an unintended result of
   that exhaustion, or its existence may be the global boundary condition that
   made the operation dominant.

## Arc landmarks

1. **Latent topology:** The defective apparatus and live controller complete
   the first anomalous cycle. The operation initially exists only as something
   the machine does.
2. **Reward loop:** Moderate pruning gives Evan real clarity and useful
   results. Each success supplies a reasonable motive for another run.
3. **Local attractor:** Evan's cognition, decisions, and immediate environment
   increasingly favor continuation. Ruth's incompatible memory and the first
   consent injuries reveal that coherence is being purchased by removing
   difference.
4. **Formalization:** Independent replication and containment turn the anomaly
   into a distributed scientific process.
5. **Independence:** The distributed system closes a live projection cycle
   without Evan or the original apparatus, before anyone has conclusive proof
   that the discarded alternatives are inhabited histories.
6. **Revelation and resistance:** The entities, degrading Quitter family, and
   impossible causal chains establish the true cost. Tess remains recognizably
   nonparallel while records and people around her converge. Shutdown attempts
   provide further consequential feedback.
7. **Separation from Evan:** Evan makes a sincere refusal and chooses helpless
   presence over another intervention. The operation routes around him through
   its independent carriers.
8. **Terminal eigenoperation:** Hardware, software, institutional process, and
   ordinary causal history become different expressions of the same closure
   pattern. The operation dominates by consuming the novelty and
   counterfactual freedom it requires.
9. **Genesis boundary:** Evan and Tess remain irreducibly distinct at the
   exhausted fixed point. Their independent final choices produce a child
   universe beginning at its own Big Bang.

## Observable signatures

- The effect follows live causal participation rather than field strength,
  physical proximity, or copied form.
- Reversing a live bit-to-action mapping reverses raw bias while preserving the
  favored physical consequence.
- Recorded and replayed decisions remain ordinary.
- Rank collapse and consequential bias appear across distributed controls after
  the original apparatus is offline.
- Separate attempts to test, restrict, or stop the effect begin reproducing the
  same decision topology.
- As closure deepens, records, language, institutions, and long causal chains
  lose irrelevant alternatives and become unnaturally efficient.

## Epiphany analogue

The operation has none. It cannot learn or change its goal.

The revelation belongs to Evan, Tess, Lena, and the reader: the thing that
appears to secure its own future is consuming the conditions under which any
future, including its own, can remain open.

## Genesis role

The operation does not build a mature successor universe. The child begins at
its own Big Bang and unfolds within its own time.

No intact person, personal memory, or usable message crosses the boundary. Evan
and Tess's irreducible relation persists only as a general time-symmetric
causal invariant embedded in the child universe's fine structure. At the end of
their existence they glimpse the universe-that-will-be from the parent side.

Much later, a person in our universe perceives the same invariant during a
DMT-like state. The mind renders it as a machine-elf-like entity conveying the
parent history symbolically. The observer forgets the usable content as the
state fades and retains only the memory of universe-scale understanding.

This is indirect contact without communication. The book does not resolve
whether recursive closure caused genesis or whether genesis was the boundary
condition shaping recursive closure.

The exact final operations, controls, hardware, and choice structure remain
deferred until the last chapter or two are outlined.

- Increasingly long chains in which every surviving detail becomes useful.
- Late repetition, compressed biography, and the disappearance of irrelevant
