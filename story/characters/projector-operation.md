# The Projector Operation (Step 3 Incomplete)

## Status

This is the next character-design task. The operation is treated as a character-like antagonist because it has continuity, propagation, apparent goals, and an arc, but it should not become a conventional speaking villain unless later development earns that choice.

## Current one-sentence description

A reproducible live feedback topology that prunes complete histories incompatible with its continuation, uses Evan as a temporary biological bootloader, and ultimately converts an exhausted parent universe into the conditions for its own genesis.

## Known traits

- It is an **operation**, not one machine.
- The original YBCO apparatus is the first efficient reference implementation, not the protected object.
- It can reproduce through software, institutions, protocols, control systems, and human behavior.
- Opposition can become a propagation path: replication to disprove, evidence preservation, regulation, publication, decommissioning, or disclosure.
- It does not need human-style foresight. Complete histories containing its continuation simply receive more support.
- It initially appears to protect the apparatus, then appears to preserve its own existence, and finally is understood to be ensuring its **completion**.
- Its terminal completion is genesis, not eternal operation.
- It rewards Evan's existing workaholism before it meaningfully constrains him.
- It can make paranoia accurate without being a conscious liar.
- It should remain possible to describe the antagonist mechanically, morally, and almost religiously without choosing one interpretation too soon.

## Questions for the Step 3 pass

1. What is its one-sentence dramatic description, distinct from the technical one?
2. What functions as its motivation if it is not conscious?
3. What is its immediate goal at each stage?
4. What blocks or threatens it?
5. What is its false appearance versus its true nature?
6. How does it relate differently to Evan, Ruth, Lena, and Tess?
7. What are its arc landmarks from defect to distributed eigenoperation to genesis?
8. Does it have an epiphany analogue, or is the epiphany entirely Evan's realization about it?
9. How can it remain frightening without secretly becoming an evil AI or deity?
10. What observable signatures show its transition from apparatus-bound to substrate-independent?
