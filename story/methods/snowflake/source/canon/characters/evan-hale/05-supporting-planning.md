# Evan Hale: Step 5 supporting planning

Status: accepted
Snowflake step: 05

This file preserves locked rationale, information boundaries, change maps,
late-bound notes, and the completed decision record behind the synopsis. It
does not replace the deliverable. The synopsis remains provisional.

## Expanded planning

Locked treatment-prose boundary:

- The synopsis carries events, observations, choices, and consequences. It
  should not stop to answer rejected interpretations, certify Evan's motives,
  defend the science before an event occurs, or state which reading the reader
  must reject.
- Explicit guardrails, superseded alternatives, science defenses, and
  late-bound rationale belong in this supporting file and their subject
  owners.
- When a boundary is necessary for continuity, the synopsis should establish
  it through what happens. Legitimate experimental negatives, unresolved
  causal distinctions, and Evan's final uncertainty remain part of the prose.
- Expansion is preferable to explanatory shorthand. This boundary is not a
  compression rule.

Locked narrative distance:

- Evan's treatment uses evolving close third in the present tense.
- The narration stays within what Evan knows and believes at each point in the
  story. His engineering habits and self-justifications shape what it treats
  as relevant.
- Irony can emerge from what Evan notices, excuses, or leaves out. The
  narration cannot use his later epiphany to explain his earlier choices for
  him.
- The treatment is neither a first-person confession nor a retrospective
  account from the terminal Evan.
- After Evan reaches the limit of what he can know, the final sentence may
  state the authorial fact that the universe ends. This deliberate terminal
  widening is not evidence available to Evan and should not be softened back
  into uncertainty.

Locked early awareness:

- Before the incompatible granddaughter memory, Evan notices each time he
  proceeds without Ruth, but he does not recognize the incidents as one
  accumulating pattern of abandonment.
- He treats each choice as temporary and technically justified. A clean run
  now seems more useful to Ruth than waiting for her, especially when the
  result might let him return with something worth sharing or something that
  could help her.
- The narration can make the repetition and growing distance visible while
  Evan still experiences each decision as a separate reasonable exception.
- This preserves his responsibility without making him cold enough to accept
  Ruth's neglect as an explicit cost.

Locked organizing spine:

- Ruth remains the emotional spine of Evan's treatment through the entire
  story. Her death does not end her structural place in his account.
- The garage work initially gives Evan a real way to remain connected to Ruth.
  It gradually replaces that connection while allowing him to believe he is
  still working for her.
- Evan's systems are a defense mechanism bounded to him. He formalizes grief,
  helplessness, and uncertainty so that he can keep working instead of facing
  what he may lose. His arc is not a general struggle over whether he should
  decide for other people.
- Evan translates Ruth's memories, Tess's survival and possible carrier role,
  the laboratory death, and Lena's objections into procedures that let him
  keep working instead of facing helplessness, grief, and uncertainty.
- The carrier is the largest expression of the same pattern. It converts every
  interruption into another continuation.
- Ruth's death lets Evan recognize his own repeated loop. Afterward, her
  absence measures whether he can remain present with Tess and Lena and
  finally act while accepting uncertainty he cannot remove.
- Tess does not replace Ruth. She is the person Evan still loves and the person
  with whom his change must become a relationship rather than another insight.

Locked central hinge:

- Ruth's incompatible granddaughter memory and Evan's concordance-window
  response form the central hinge of his treatment.
- The memory alone is not the turn. The turn is Evan answering a lucid,
  coherent Ruth who disagrees with his history by defining a successful result
  as autobiographical agreement with his own records.
- In Evan's immediate account, concordance is a necessary control that might
  preserve the benefit while removing an apparent overshoot. The treatment
  can make the moral change legible without giving him that language yet.
- The later erasure proof establishes that Ruth's contradiction was access to
  a real inhabited history already being stripped away. The concordance
  protocol was never an identity test. It would suppress a real part of her
  autobiography because it disagreed with Evan.
- This is where his pattern shifts from letting work displace Ruth to turning
  her unsettling memory into a parameter he can control. The procedure lets
  him avoid the uncertainty in her account and continue working inside a
  system he understands.

Locked first clarity reward:

- Evan's treatment gives the first clarity state two surviving technical
  results. A better phase sequence improves the ordinary flux pump enough to
  keep the defective ring useful and moves the cold notch between installed
  sensors, where it appears to vanish.
- Near zero speed, the motor controller has two valid electrical-angle
  possibilities and insufficient back electromotive force to distinguish
  them. Evan realizes the estimator must preserve both until the signal
  returns rather than force one.
- The flux-pump improvement and motor fix both hold up after the clarity fades.
  This gives Evan a sound reason to trust the state and claim the insights as
  his own.
- Related altered states recur during later timing work. Evan does not connect
  the first epiphanies to the garage, but repetition eventually makes the
  connection impossible to dismiss.
- The parallel between preserving alternatives in the motor controller and
  destroying them through the projector remains visible to the reader but
  unstated by Evan.
- Evan is a mostly remote Controls Engineer at the fictional Alder Motion
  Controls, a Portland-area maker of industrial motor controllers and
  servo-drive electronics. He has substantial accrued paid time off and uses
  it openly for family care. There is no employer conflict or day-job crisis.
- Implementation detail beyond the two hypotheses and the surrounding work
  scene remain outside the treatment unless later needed.

Locked pre-hinge balance:

- The treatment uses three technical strokes before the central hinge.
  Evan reinstalls the defective ring and reproduces the cold notch, his
  phase-sequence and motor-control insights survive outside the clarity state,
  and fresh randomness favors a physical consequence only when it controls a
  live action.
- The live-consequence result gives Evan a reason to call Lena. The treatment
  compresses the mapping reversals, blinded operator-effect work, and later
  rare-target validation rather than reproducing the full control ladder.
- The saved space belongs to Tess's arrival as an independent auditor and to
  Ruth's requested lucid intervals. Evan must experience the team as having
  good reason to continue before the granddaughter contradiction.
- Ruth enters Tess's audit as a colleague before the lucid intervals. One
  plain mathematical question exposes a real flaw in Tess's proposed control.
  Tess revises it and brings the new version back to Ruth before the run. The
  exact question and flaw remain late-bound.
- During Ruth's requested repeat runs, late sessions become overnight stays for
  Tess and Mouse in the spare room. Ruth tells Tess to stop carrying Mouse's

  begun rather than causing the first romantic turn.
- During that later conversation, Tess's age and account connect her to the

  tells her immediately, and she corrects the simplified miracle story he
  remembers. His later secret record search begins only after he suspects her
  causal rarity may matter.
- This balance preserves Evan's technical attention without allowing
  laboratory procedure to displace the relationship the treatment is about.

Locked Tess-source hypothesis:

- As responses around the apparatus become easier to predict, Tess's continued
  variability and the improbable history behind her survival make Evan suspect
  that her presence and choices may be affecting the developing operation.
- Tess has already spent months auditing the work, altering interpretations,
  and affecting which tests run before Evan forms this hypothesis. Her possible
  causal participation therefore precedes his decision to measure it.
- Lena's hardware-independent analogue later reproduces the effect and altered
  state without Evan or Tess present. This rules both of them out as necessary
  causes of the base phenomenon.
- The independent result cannot establish whether Tess's earlier presence and
  decisions helped this particular network become a carrier.
- After he suspects that possibility, Evan secretly logs her choices, mines
  the records of her family deaths, rescue, and treatment, and keeps her in the
  work while he tries to isolate her resistance as a signal.
- His questions around small decisions become more particular, and he
  sometimes records Tess's answers afterward. She notices, but Evan already
  logs nearly everything, her judgments legitimately affect the work, and they
  are newly intimate. She reads the attention as ordinary documentation mixed
  with genuine interest. Evan lets that reasonable interpretation stand.
- His voluntary admission later gives those moments a second meaning. Tess
  cannot know which ordinary exchanges were also observations. The affection
  remains genuine; the violation is that he allowed it to double as
  undisclosed measurement.
- Tess's first response is, "What exactly did you use?" She requires Evan to
  identify what he took from her life before the argument turns to his reasons.
  Evan starts with the ferry, rescue, and medical records, the facts he took
  from them, and the rarity estimate he built. Only then does he name the
  ordinary choices he isolated and which garage observations and runs the
  analysis affected. He knows which part of the confession cuts deepest and
  does not make Tess force him past a technical account. The exact documents
  and details remain late-bound.
- Evan's copies of the records, his model and calculations, and the
  Tess-specific choice logs remain on a garage workstation and local storage
  separate from the apparatus controller. He sends none of it, including any
  derived Tess-specific conclusion, to Lena or the participating laboratories.
  It does not enter the proof-to-stop protocol. Evan uses it only to guide his
  private interpretation and local garage runs before he confesses. The
  workstation and storage are not part of the active core he later moves.
- The first record Evan names is the official rescue timeline. It reconstructs
  the search attempts, failed approaches, the temporary path that let rescuers
  reach Tess, her extraction, and the attempts to reach the rest of her family.
  As he begins to recite it, Tess stops him: "I know the timeline. Tell me what
  you did with it." She has studied the document herself and makes Evan account
  for his action rather than repeat her history. He tells her that he used the
  sequence to model the rescue bottleneck that left Tess alive when the others
  were not. Exact agency, title, timings, access path, and rescue mechanics
  remain late-bound.
- Tess then says, "Show me." Evan opens the complete local folder on the garage
  workstation. She reviews the source records, model, calculations, timestamped
  choice logs, and notes linking them to particular garage observations and
  runs. She verifies the scope without moving or copying the folder out of the
  garage.
- Evan stays beside Tess, answers every question directly, does not choose the
  file order, and does not touch the keyboard unless she asks him to open
  something. His cooperation gives the voluntary confession substance without
  repairing the breach. The treatment does not need to specify a first file or
  narrate the review screen by screen.
- The breach begins with that concealment and continued use after suspicion.
  It is not that Evan knowingly caused her initial participation or made her
  the source of the phenomenon.
- The independent analogue rules Tess out as necessary for the basic effect,
  but it cannot settle whether her earlier choices helped this projector
  lineage become an independent carrier. Evan's records show that he suspected
  the possibility while Tess could still have withdrawn from live work. By his
  confession, withdrawal cannot undo her possible contribution.
- Tess's organizing response is fear that she may have helped the anomaly get
  out of control, together with upset that Evan withheld the risk and denied
  her the chance to decide whether to continue. The private use of her survival
  records deepens the breach, but fury is not its emotional center.
- Calling their meeting inevitable is a poor attempt to explain himself, but
  it is not the basis of the breach and does not destroy their love.

Locked early contact information boundary:

- During longer runs, Evan retains the impression of a bounded form that
  converts every attempted interruption into its next movement. He privately

- Evan tells Lena about the surviving impression and converted-interruption

- Evan initially treats the form as an embodiment of the carrier. This is a

Locked post-hinge balance:

- The treatment uses four post-hinge turns.
- After Lena refuses, Evan sponsors the proof-to-stop test through consortium
  approval. Its initial result appears negative, and the precommitted
  containment response completes the independent carrier it was meant to
  detect.
- Paired loss and echo reveals erased histories. Evan admits that, after he
  suspected Tess's decisions might be helping this particular carrier form, he
  measured them and kept using her work without telling her. The breach
  frightens her with the possibility that she helped the anomaly escape the
  garage and seriously damages her trust because he denied her a chance to
  withdraw. It does not end their love.
- Evan moves the original apparatus core away from Ruth and Tess for a remote
  shutdown attempt. His own safety condition makes the ringless garage part of
  the live response. Its destruction triggers a fallback cycle, and he returns
  after Ruth's death.
- Evan recognizes the same motion in his marriage, Ruth's lost time, and the
  unfinished shutdown waiting for him. He sees that work has repeatedly let
  him hide from loss, grief, and even their possibility. He consciously chooses
  a different course by turning toward Tess and the life immediately in front
  of them.
- The journey to Lena and the terminal choice test whether that decision
  changes more than one moment. Evan seeks Lena as a person, honors her last
  refusal, and finally makes his own choice without knowing what Tess will do.
  Mouse remains present as the last shared external reference.
- Technical carrier detail and cosmic escalation remain legible through these
  decisions without taking over the treatment.

Locked return epiphany:

- When Evan returns after Ruth's death, he briefly recognizes the same motion
  in the miscarriage planter, the collapse of his marriage, and the time he
  has lost with Ruth. Pain arrives, and he turns away from the person beside
  him to work on it.
- He recognizes that the powered-down remote apparatus, its logs, and the
  unfinished shutdown are offering him the same escape. Work has let him avoid
  facing loss and grief, and avoid remaining present when either one is still
  only a possibility.
- The recognition becomes consequential through a positive choice. He turns
  toward Tess and the immediate human work of Ruth's burial, survival, and
  shared aftermath rather than disappearing into the next problem.
- This is not a symbolic prohibition on all later work or investigation. It is
  the first time Evan sees his own loop while he is entering it and
  consciously chooses another course.
- This recognition begins a real change but does not prove it complete. The
  journey to Lena and his inability to know Tess's final choice still have to
  test whether he can remain present and act without certainty.
- The recognition does not redeem Ruth's lost time, his marriage, the
  miscarriage, or any erased history.

Locked confession understanding:

- Evan understands that Tess's participation began before he suspected a
  special causal role for her. He did not knowingly place her inside the
  initial operation.
- He also understands what he chose afterward. He treated her resistance as a
  signal, searched the records of her family catastrophe, and continued using
  her audits while concealing the possibility that they were helping shape the
  carrier.
- Evan confesses because the concealment can no longer coexist with the truth
  they now share. He still reaches first for a clean causal explanation and
  says that their meeting may have been inevitable.
- Tess distinguishes the uncertainty before his suspicion from his deliberate
  secrecy after it. His inevitability remark fails because it does not address
  that distinction, not because inevitability itself makes their relationship
  false.
- The admission seriously damages her trust. It does not end their love or
  settle whether her earlier presence affected this carrier.
- His honesty matters, but it cannot undo the breach or substitute explanation
  for the slower work of remaining present with her afterward.

Locked post-incident understanding:

- Evan accepts both casualties as real. He does not minimize the death or
  dismiss the survivor's selective autobiographical loss.
- He classifies them differently. The death is an engineering and containment
  failure. The memory loss is specific enough to trouble him, but the physical
  trauma leaves it medically confounded.
- His compliance with the evidence hold is sincere. He powers down the garage
  because continuing under the failed safeguards would be indefensible, even
  though nobody can force him to stop.
- Once the investigation permits a bounded restart, Evan believes continued
  measurement under stronger controls is the responsible way to find the
  failure and prevent another death.
- This is another form of his central mistake. Faced with grief and
  uncertainty, he converts them into a better procedure. The procedure is
  reasonable enough that he does not yet have to see the pattern in himself.
- He does not privately know that the survivor's loss is cosmological. The
  later paired loss and echo remains the point at which medical ambiguity
  fails and erasure becomes the best explanation.

Locked selected-site role:

- Evan joins the selected-site restart as a technical adviser. He has full
  access to shared measurements, controller logs, and containment records.
- He helps compare runs, interpret anomalies, and design safeguards. He does
  not chair the consortium, operate a participating site's apparatus, or hold
  unilateral authority to approve a consequential run.
- Local teams retain physical control of their systems. Consequential tests
  require the consortium's shared approval under the containment plan.
- When Lena refuses the proof-to-stop test, Evan formulates and sponsors the
  proposal through that approval process. Other researchers and institutions
  authorize and execute it. He does not hack a replica, bypass a hard trip, or
  restart the garage apparatus.
- Lena can refuse her participation and endorsement, but she does not hold a
  unilateral veto over the other sites. Evan's decision to proceed remains a
  breach of their collaboration even though it follows formal procedure.
- The institutional route is part of the trap. The proposal, review,
  approvals, site actions, measurements, and containment responses can become
  components of the live carrier they are trying to test.
- Exact committee structure, vote, participating site, and experimental
  implementation remain late-bound.

Locked proof-to-stop disclosure:

- Evan's proposal states the carrier hypothesis and the recursive danger
  plainly. The test result will determine containment or decommissioning
  actions, and that live response may strengthen or complete the suspected
  carrier.
- The proposal also records Lena's refusal and her reason for it. Evan does not
  recast her objection as a generic request for more caution.
- Consortium reviewers, participating site leads, and the operators who will
  execute the test receive the same material before approval. Uncertainty is
  marked as uncertainty rather than softened or concealed.
- They approve knowingly. Suspicion alone will not sustain permanent shutdown,
  and separate sites making uncoordinated choices may create a worse risk than
  one bounded test under the common containment plan.
- Their consent is ethically real. It does not protect the decision from
  becoming a live input to the operation. The informed review, approval, site
  actions, and response are the very structure the test is meant to examine.
- Evan treats full disclosure and collective approval as evidence that the
  test is legitimate. This preserves a distinction from his secret use of
  Tess's choices and records, even though both acts express his habit of
  converting danger and uncertainty into a procedure he knows how to operate.
- Exact briefing language, review sequence, vote, and operator discussion
  remain late-bound.

Locked proof-to-stop response:

- The test's initial measurements fail to show a self-sustaining carrier. Under
  the approved protocol, that negative result triggers coordinated containment
  and decommissioning actions across the participating sites.
- Those actions change conditions elsewhere and determine what other systems
  do next. Only the complete protocol, including its response, closes the first
  independent cycle. The aftermath proves the carrier by showing that the test
  completed it.
- Evan understands the sequence correctly. The informed approval process, the
  initial negative result, and the participating sites' promised response were
  all parts of the carrier.
- His first reaction is grim vindication rather than triumph or immediate
  remorse. Lena was right about the recursive danger. Evan was right that the
  institutions would not sustain permanent shutdown without proof.
- He still believes seeking the proof was necessary. In his account, the test
  was dangerous, but ignorance and fragmented site decisions would have been
  worse.
- He moves directly into coordinated shutdown work. He wants to map the live
  links, prevent further consequential feedback, and give each site a
  decommissioning path that does not create another response cycle.
- He does not yet regret sponsoring the test. Its actionable aftermath lets
  him treat the damage as the cost of learning enough to act, which is another
  version of converting helplessness into work.
- This is not his epiphany. The later erasure proof changes what he thinks the
  operation costs, and Ruth's death finally changes what he does when another
  investigation presents itself.
- Exact reporting sequence, shutdown plan, and first post-result action remain
  late-bound.

Locked understanding of Lena's rupture:

- Evan expects the collaboration to survive the test. He disclosed Lena's
  objection accurately, the result proved her concern correct, and he now sees
  coordinated containment as a duty they share.
- He expects anger, damaged trust, and a hard argument. He does not expect Lena
  to end their active work together.
- When she withdraws from every live response, Evan recognizes that she has
  drawn a moral boundary. In the treatment's close-third compression, `Lena
  flees` names the shock of her physical departure from Evan's position. It
  does not mean she panics, misunderstands the evidence, or abandons her
  agency.
- He nevertheless experiences the departure as leaving when the work matters
  most. In his account, the dangerous test has created an obligation to help
  contain what it proved.
- He does not attack Lena publicly or misstate her refusal. His mistake is
  expecting accurate acknowledgment and a shared problem to preserve a
  relationship after he acted across its boundary.
- He cannot yet understand her withdrawal as a decision that the work itself
  has become the carrier. His later recognition of his own repeated loop
  alters that reading before he honors her final refusal at the observatory.
- Exact exchange, timing, and practical separation remain late-bound.

Locked remote shutdown and local catastrophe:

- The original ring alone cannot operate. Evan moves the minimum active core
  needed for a live run: the defective ring, LN2 stage, thermal and RF
  drive, sensors, controller state, and consequential feedback path.
- He takes the core to a remote industrial or test location because he believes
  physical danger will remain near the energized hardware and participating
  laboratories. Exact site, transport, and supporting utilities remain
  late-bound.
- Evan's planned decommissioning warms the ring through its superconducting
  transition, lets the pinned-flux state decay, and opens the local feedback
  loop while participating sites begin synchronized decommissioning. The YBCO
  ring operates under LN2, so this is not a magnet-style quench. Ordinary
  hazards remain in nitrogen handling, vessel pressure, RF and electrical
  drive, and mechanical failure.
- A monitored safety state at the garage and house is part of the precommitted
  decision map. If it disappears, the sites halt decommissioning and enter a
  fallback verification run. Evan adds this condition to protect Ruth and Tess.
- Before Evan leaves for the remote site, he kisses Ruth on the top of the
  head, pets Mouse, and says, "Love you, I'll be back." This is their last
  meaningful exchange.
- That condition makes the ringless garage causally consequential. Its
  historical importance and physical proximity would not be enough.
- During the remote procedure, an improbable but physically ordinary
  infrastructure and subsurface chain destroys the garage, isolates the
  surviving house, and removes the safety state. Existing geologic or
  industrial energy supplies the collapse and blast.
- The loss triggers the fallback response, closes another distributed cycle,
  and supplies the state for the next one. The operation does not target Ruth
  or Tess, move energy from the ring to the house, or act from a private wish.
- Evan aborts the remote procedure and returns. The original apparatus remains
  powered down at the remote site.

Locked public centrality:

- Evan does not receive a conventional fame arc.
- The world increasingly bends around him because his continued participation
  is one condition of the final carrier loop. Access, institutional attention,
  and other people's locally motivated actions converge around his position.
- This is causal centrality rather than celebrity, destiny, or selection by a
  private intelligence. The mindless projector operation remains the attractor.
- His former wife does not appear in the present timeline. She remains part of
  his remembered history and the pattern he recognizes after Ruth's death.

Locked later understanding of Lena's refusal:

Locked consequence and limit of Ruth's lucid intervals:

- The later paired loss and echo changes the meaning of Ruth's incompatible
  granddaughter memory. It was access to an inhabited history being removed.
- Evan's concordance window would have treated that real autobiographical
  distinction as an error and selected for agreement with his own records.
  This is the moral consequence of the protocol.
- Ruth's interior experience remains unavailable to Evan. He never learns what
  the lucid intervals felt like to her, whether she experienced them as rescue,
  or what private meaning she gave the granddaughter memory and the recovered
  time. He does know that she rejected autobiographical concordance as the
  target and expressly requested one final reduced exposure under her terms.
- Evan can understand what his concordance window would have done. He cannot
  work backward from that recognition and turn Ruth's stated consent into an
  account of her unmeasured experience or subjective benefit.
- Tess's unobserved final choice and the operation's terminal status remain
  real uncertainties, but they do not replace Ruth as the emotional limit of
  his account.
- Ruth leaves Evan a human message of love through Tess. The treatment cannot
  turn that message into retrospective approval, cosmological authority, or a
  resolution of Evan's guilt. Ruth's explanation of his lifelong retreat into
  work gives Tess context but does not cause the self-recognition Evan reaches
  through his own conduct after Ruth's death.

Locked return of the original apparatus:

- Evan and Tess do not retrieve the remote rig before going to Lena. Evan goes
  because Lena is there, and Tess goes because Lena is her friend.
- During stable-geometric failure, the remote test site becomes physically

  powered-down original apparatus and defective ring accessible for the final
  act without installing a machine at the receive-only station.
- The original ring is not a unique cosmic key. Independent replicas already
  prove that the effect does not require it. Its return matters because it is

  carried Evan's failed attempt to protect Ruth into the ending.
- Exact apparatus condition, overlap geometry, restart sequence, terminal
  controls, and genesis mechanism remain late-bound.

Locked change map:

- **Connection:** The garage project begins as a genuine way for Evan to remain
  connected to Ruth.
- **Substitution:** The project gradually replaces that connection while
  allowing him to keep calling the work an act of care.
- **Formalization:** He translates Ruth's memories, Tess's survival and
  possible carrier role, the laboratory death, and Lena's objections into
  procedures he knows how to operate. The procedures let him keep working
  instead of facing helplessness, grief, and uncertainty.
- **Carrier:** The independent operation magnifies his pattern. Every attempt
  to interrupt it becomes the condition for another continuation.
- **Recognition and presence:** Ruth's death lets Evan see his own loop while
  another technical problem is offering him escape. He consciously turns
  toward Tess and remains with the life in front of him.
- **Uncertainty:** His change persists with Lena and reaches its final test with
  Tess. Evan makes the terminal choice without knowing hers and accepts that
  love cannot supply proof against loss.
