# Current Canon

This file records the story decisions currently accepted after restarting the Snowflake process. It outranks the legacy outline and draft.

## Working title

Open. `Causal Flux`, `The Causal Projector`, and other earlier labels remain working names only.

## Intended form and tone

- Hard science fiction escalating into ontological horror and cosmic tragedy.
- Conceptually dense, clear, dry, intimate, and technically literate.
- Similar ambitions to qntm-style fiction, but do not imitate any living author's prose.
- The science must generate character choices and consequences rather than becoming a sequence of impressive laboratory reports.

## Snowflake Step 1: one-sentence summary

> A workaholic engineer caring for his fading mother discovers that his harmless garage project might restore her mind, and in trying to perfect it sacrifices first their remaining time together, then the universe itself.

## Snowflake Step 2: five-sentence summary

1. **Months into caring for his mother Ruth, controls engineer Evan Hale is building a garage-scale MRI flux source with her when a defective superconducting ring begins producing measurements that should not agree.**

2. **While testing the anomaly, Evan experiences impossible clarity and discovers that the machine can force the improbable to happen, turning the project he shares with Ruth into an obsession that steals their remaining time together.**

3. **When Evan exposes Ruth to the machine, she becomes lucid and casually asks after the granddaughter who survived in her memories but was miscarried in his; Evan treats the impossible detail as a calibration error rather than confront what the machine has selected.**

4. **Addicted to the machine and haunted by entities that prove to be echoes of dying realities, Evan realizes each run is erasing entire universes; yet every attempt to stop becomes another impossible link in the chain that keeps it operating while Ruth's last lucid hours pass without him.**

5. **As reality collapses around them into a fractal singularity, Evan and Esther "Tess" Kertesz, the one person too causally rare to be smoothly rewritten, make a final independent choice intended to erase his mistake, only for their act to become the genesis of our universe.**

## Ending planning status

The broad ending remains that Evan and Tess attempt to end the projector and their independent final choices become the genesis of our universe. The mutual-veto assurance game, noncommuting controls, and exact factorization mechanism are **favored hypotheses, not locked chapter mechanics**. Joe does not want the terminal operation fully engineered until the final chapter or two has been earned by the preceding plot. Preserve the emotional constraints and seed the relevant invariants; leave the actual procedure late-bound.

## Core tragic engine

Evan does not begin by trying to cure Ruth. He is already living with her and caring for her. The garage project gives them something to work on and talk about while she begins to disappear. He never seriously expects it to restore her mind.

The tragedy begins when the project produces real evidence that it might.

Evan's fatal strategy is to turn helplessness, grief, and love into work. He believes that labor is how he proves care. The machine rewards exactly this pathology by making his labor effective, euphoric, and increasingly inevitable.

The novel's private thematic sentence is:

> Evan cannot accept helpless presence as a form of love.

## Opening and timeline

- Begin with Evan and Ruth already working in the garage, near the point when the experiment becomes strange.
- Do not open with his move into the house, the end of his marriage, or a long apparatus origin story.
- Reveal backstory through present objects, habits, flashbacks, and memory.
- The opening project is an ordinary engineering attempt to create a persistent flux source for portable or open low-field MRI without liquid helium.
- Evan has built an air liquefier using a cryocooler salvaged from an old cellular rack, a detail drawn from Joe's real experience.

## Ruth in the garage

Ruth is a retired mathematician and Evan's assistant.

- During lucid periods she understands the work and occasionally supplies decisive insights.
- Her dementia fluctuates enough that on some days she knows Evan is familiar
  but cannot clearly place him as her son.
- Evan keeps her involved for companionship and gives her bounded jobs so she
  can feel useful and remain a participant. He checks all of her work himself.
- Whenever the cold notch appears in data she is reviewing, Ruth notices and
  reports it as though it were the first occurrence.
- The repetition is not a mystical benefit or a formal blinded control. It
  shows that her mathematical habits survive even when her memory of prior
  runs does not.
- Ruth's first projector-linked lucidity occurs incidentally while she is
  performing a real task in a live run. She is causally participating, not
  affected by proximity alone, and nobody has proposed a cognitive experiment
  on her.
- Ruth then requests reduced repeat exposures. Evan, Lena, and the rest of the
  team initially support the runs. She is not deceived or treated over an
  objection.
- The intervals deepen with repetition. Each run restores more present
  orientation, working memory, conversational continuity, and autobiographical
  reach, and each interval lasts longer than the one before it.
- The gains remain temporary. Ruth returns to her ordinary fluctuating
  impairment after each interval.
- The first exposure and early repeats contain no incompatible
  autobiographical details or smaller warning contradictions.
- The granddaughter memory appears during Ruth's clearest and longest interval
  yet, as an ordinary fact within an otherwise coherent state. It is what first
  makes Lena object to continuing human runs.
- Evan calls the interval an overshoot and proposes finding a concordance
  window: the strongest lucid state in which Ruth's autobiography still agrees
  with his record family.
- Agreement with Evan's history becomes his operational standard for the
  correct Ruth. Lena objects that this can select for conformity but cannot
  authenticate identity.
- Evan converts that objection into a demand for better controls and begins
  formalizing another reduced run. Ruth's response to the proposed run remains
  open, and her consent is not automatic.
- Ruth's final lucidity is natural rather than projector-generated. It is
  terminal or paradoxical lucidity, followed by increasing tiredness, trouble
  speaking and swallowing, intermittent responsiveness, unconsciousness, and
  death with Tess present. Earlier material must foreshadow worsening fatigue,
  mobility, eating, and swallowing.
- The operation causes Evan's absence and the collapse of practical support
  around Ruth. It does not cause her final lucidity or medically cause her
  death.
- Tess remains fully present with Ruth while angry with Evan. The exact final
  exchange remains open.
- Evan's long science explanations are one of the ways they spend time together and one of the ways he performs intimacy.
- A couch has been moved into the garage. Ruth often listens from it, sleeps there, or wakes with an unexpectedly sharp observation.
- Sometimes she loses the thread, wanders into the garden, or falls asleep while Evan continues explaining.
- At first, Evan treats these departures as pauses in their shared project. He
  waits, finds her, or catches her up.
- Once the anomaly becomes rewarding, he continues through her absences. He
  does not ban her from the work. The project simply advances without her
  until her participation becomes incidental.
- The garage initially represents companionship. Later Evan abandons Ruth emotionally while remaining in the same room with the machine.
- The kitchen remains useful for medication, meals, caregiving friction, practical independence, and the parts of life that do not fit the project.

## Evan's old wound

Years earlier, Evan's wife miscarried.

- She cried in the bathroom.
- Evan yelled from pain rather than anger; the distinction did not make the experience less damaging.
- They retrieved the miscarriage from the toilet and buried it in a planter.
- Flowers grew, but both of them neglected the planter while Evan buried himself in work.
- His workaholism and emotional absence contributed to the marriage's collapse, although the marriage should not be treated as a lost ideal family he must recover.
- The book is not a search for a daughter he never knew.
- Ruth's alternate memory of a surviving granddaughter is a concrete, painful discrepancy, not the beginning of an alternate-child quest.
- The dead flowers can return later as Evan recognizes that he is repeating the same response: working through grief while the person beside him needs presence.

## Underlying cosmology

- Reality is a finite, locally discrete causal structure.
- The multiverse is the finite set of globally consistent complete histories, not an infinite branching tree.
- The apparent Hilbert space contains many formal directions that cannot be embedded in any valid complete history.
- Valid physical states occupy a lower-dimensional subset of the mathematical state space.
- The apparatus rotates a small component of the local future toward unsupported directions. Global consistency projects that component out and renormalizes what remains.
- The machine cannot create impossible outcomes. It changes the support of already valid outcomes.
- A jointly extraordinary outcome does not require ordinary coordination among
  its participants. The operation can select a valid complete history in which
  many people independently make the same physically available choice. The
  improbability belongs to the conjunction, not to any individual's action.
  This does not imply telepathy, possession, or communication from the
  operation.
- Branch multiplicity is the weighted number of valid histories supporting a local state or macroscopic identity.

## Identity and dementia

- A person is a redundant bundle of nearby implementations across valid histories.
- This redundancy acts like error correction and supplies a counterfactual reserve.
- Moderate reduction suppresses noise and competing weak trajectories, producing focus, confidence, and flow.
- Severe reduction removes flexibility, correction, and fine distinctions, producing rigidity, confabulation, aliasing, and identity collapse.
- Dementia in the fictional theory is uneven loss of overlap among the history bundles supporting memory, language, recognition, emotion, and selfhood.
- Ruth's deepening lucid intervals are meaningful temporary benefits, but they
  do not prove restoration. The later interval selects a coherent
  implementation that contains memories absent from Evan's record family.
- Evan's proposed concordance window can measure agreement with his record
  family. It cannot establish that a matching implementation is the original
  or correct Ruth.
- This is fictional cosmology, not a claim about real dementia.

## The apparatus

Real-science ancestry:

- melt-textured YBCO high-temperature superconductor;
- type-II vortices and flux pinning;
- a thermally actuated traveling-wave flux pump;
- a Prussian-blue magnetic material whose permeability changes with temperature;
- a moving heat pulse producing a moving magnetic-reluctance / field profile;
- repeated vortex injection and pinning;
- an asymmetric coherent-plus-lossy YBCO structure;
- rotating or traveling RF magnetic drive;
- ordinary energy and entropy costs.

Fictional leap:

- a material defect and control-system phase edge cause the ordinary electromagnetic cycle nearly to close while leaving a small geometric mismatch in the deeper causal code;
- repeated cycles act like a non-Hermitian projector or power iteration over complete histories;
- the live feedback path selects which future components survive.

Opening diagnostic sequence:

- Evan and Ruth are trying to pump enough trapped field into a superconducting
  ring to justify continued work toward a portable imaging device.
- The old defective ring falls just short of that target.
- Glass-bodied silicon PN junction diodes at selected positions around the
  ring monitor the traveling thermal pulse through the Prussian-blue compound.
  They are biased at constant current, and their forward voltage supplies the
  temperature proxy. Exact part number, probe count, and attachment geometry
  remain late-bound.
- As the traveling heat pulse reaches one fixed sector of the defective ring,
  its diode reports a repeatable cold notch below the cold-stage temperature
  while neighboring probes warm normally. Evan assumes an unresolved
  instrumentation fault and replaces the suspected probe and the measurement
  chain through the data logger. He has not yet replaced the computer.
- The expensive custom ring he ordered months earlier finally arrives, meets
  the trapped-field target, and makes the temperature anomaly disappear.
- Late that night, Evan puts the defective ring back into the apparatus. The
  same anomaly returns.
- The exact depth and duration of the notch remain open. It must defeat known
  cryogenic sensor and material effects without establishing a literal
  violation of thermodynamics. Phase collapse remains a fallback if the
  temperature anomaly fails that standard, but it is not part of the current
  opening.

Podkletnov:

- The novel should explicitly mention the disputed Podkletnov rotating-YBCO experiments at some point.
- They are historical inspiration or a false interpretation, not accepted evidence of gravitational shielding.

## Discovery and validation

Strong devices worth preserving from the old outline and draft:

- the well-made replacement YBCO ring meets the engineering target and removes
  the temperature anomaly, while reinstalling the defective ring reproduces
  the anomaly;
- statistically independent sensors lose effective rank and share one residual mode;
- the control-system timing fault walks through RF, thermal, and vortex-relaxation phase;
- fresh randomness remains fair when logged, ignored, or replayed, but becomes biased when it controls a live consequential branch;
- reversing the bit-to-action mapping reverses the raw bit bias while preserving the favored physical action;
- a precommitted improbable but physically valid target occurs;
- an impossible target fails through adjacent legal nuisances, heat, and saturation rather than law violation;
- blinded independent replication separates the effect from Evan's expectation.

These experimental devices are salvageable, but the old chapter sequence is not binding.

Paragraph 2's first deliberate outcome test is locked at the level needed for
the Step 4 synopsis:

- A fresh physical random bit chooses between two safe, prepared actions in
  the live apparatus. Exact hardware, action pair, run count, and statistical
  threshold remain late-bound.
- Fresh bits remain fair when they are merely recorded or ignored. Replaying
  an earlier bitstream through the same command path produces no effect. Bias
  appears only when a fresh bit controls a real physical consequence in the
  current run.
- Swapping the bit-to-action mapping swaps the raw bit bias while preserving
  the favored physical action.
- The resulting sequence is improbable but physically valid. It establishes
  selection by consequence rather than an intrinsically lucky bit value or a
  faulty random source.
- The later precommitted rare target remains a separate escalation.

## Drug and addiction progression

- The first exposure is mental quiet, not speed.
- Each observation seems to have one correct successor.
- Evan solves real problems and gains objective benefits.
- The first externally validated benefit is a solution to an intermittent
  zero-speed motor-control failure at work. When back electromotive force
  disappears into noise, two opposing electrical-angle hypotheses remain
  compatible with the measurements. The old estimator forces one answer and
  can produce a torque spike.
- During clarity, Evan realizes that the estimator must carry both hypotheses
  through the unobservable interval, defer commitment until the signal
  returns, and trip if they do not rejoin within tolerance.
- The fix uses existing information, survives simulation and independent
  testing after the clarity state ends, and does not supply Evan with facts he
  could not have known.
- Evan initially understands the clarity as his own epiphany or flow state. He
  does not suspect that the apparatus has changed his cognition.
- This retains a concept from the legacy draft. Its scene structure, prose,
  employer context, and exact placement must be substantially rewritten.
- The live consequential-bit result convinces Evan that the apparatus has
  exposed new physics. That physical claim is why he calls Lena into the
  project. He is not asking her to investigate his mental state.
- Lena is the first to connect Evan's flow state to the apparatus. She aligns
  his operator log and spoken notes with the sensor-rank collapse, asks him
  what he experienced, and confirms the timing in a blinded repeat.
- They treat `operator effect` as a working description. The evidence shows a
  correlation, not enhancement, mind control, or a mechanism, and it does not
  authenticate Evan's subjective certainty.
- The state is euphoric because prediction error, indecision, and competing interpretations vanish.
- Later use becomes rigid, hallucinogenic, and self-confirming.
- The addiction is partly psychological and partly ontological: histories in which Evan stops receive less support than histories in which he activates the machine again.
- He can understand the mechanism and still be unable to stop because successful abstinence increasingly belongs to dying worlds.

## DMT and residual entities

- DMT does not cause the machine effect or prove supernatural beings.
- It loosens the brain's ordinary single-world / single-self decoder and makes residual branch structure perceptible.
- Entities are compressed families of alternate versions or descendant implementations of a causal pattern, not simple one-to-one doubles.
- Deep contact is not organized as an exotic place containing recognizable
  creatures. Ordinary distinctions such as location, object, body, inside,
  outside, before, and after may fail to apply.
- Evan can experience relational directions unavailable to ordinary
  perception, analogous to being able to look ana-ward or kata-ward through an
  extra spatial dimension. This analogy describes the experience, not an
  established literal geometry.
- Imagery, color, and sound occur as perturbations in experienced reality
  rather than as scenery or properties belonging to stable objects. They are
  sensory cross-sections of a change the ordinary human model cannot represent
  directly.
- An entity is recognized as a coherent agency, attention, or relation across
  transformations for which human perception has no native symbols. It need
  not possess a stable body, boundary, scale, or position.
- Fractal figures, rooms, faces, machines, and sequences belong mainly to
  memory's lossy reconstruction after contact. They are not a complete or
  literal account of what was present.
- Cosmological lore can arrive as simultaneous relational understanding rather
  than speech or a sequence of propositions. Its felt certainty does not
  authenticate its claims.
- As support is lost, recursion terminates at shallower levels. Later
  recollection becomes more repetitive, less differentiated, and less able to
  recover biography.
- Different entities have different incentives. Advice is survivor lobbying, not omniscient revelation.
- The Ruined Sovereign is retained as the recurring encounter-avatar of the
  projector operation. The Guide is its early, richly differentiated
  presentation, not a separate entity.
- The Quitter is not an entity. Stopped counterfactuals remain available as an
  unnamed encounter phenomenon: histories and relations continuing without
  returning to the projector.
- Their shared nonparticipation does not bind them into one agency, voice, or
  intention. A recovered memory may personify the contact, but no recurring
  identity or named replacement inhabits it.
- This contact can preserve evidence and moral injury without turning Evan's
  eventual refusal into a verdict delivered by the cosmos.
- The Turning Knot is retained as the second true recurring entity. It is a
  coherent family of histories carried on a nonhuman substrate incompatible
  with the one in which those histories formed. It is not the projector
  operation or a human counterfactual.
- Hold the target roster to two. Add another true entity only if a later story
  function cannot be carried by either retained entity or by an unnamed
  encounter phenomenon.
- Near genesis, recognizable selves compress into invariants such as winding, chirality, knots, phase classes, and open or closed relations.

### The Ruined Sovereign

- Its substrate is the lineage-spanning projector operation as implemented
  across people, apparatus, software, institutions, and later carriers. It
  does not add a mind, controller, or separate residual family to the
  operation.
- It is Evan-shaped because Evan's habit of converting helplessness into
  another operation is the local relation through which he encounters it. The
  resemblance is causal, not necessarily visual.
- Its motivation-equivalent is recursive closure. It is internally
  non-halting because its transition rule contains no chosen stop, but it can
  still end when no counterfactual support remains for another cycle.
- Its ruined state is expressed through movement rather than a damaged body.
  Early transformations feel almost infinite in complexity while remaining
  phenomenological and compatible with a finite multiverse. Each movement
  makes a new meaningful distinction.
- Across later contacts, independent degrees of movement disappear.
  Transformations cease opening new relations and increasingly return to the
  same relation.
- Evan's late description does not require a new figure or symbolic costume.
  He recognizes the same Guide, ruined somehow. Everything that once made its
  transformations unique has been flattened until it feels like a sovereign
  without a subject.
- Its recognition invariant is the converted interruption. Every encounter
  contains something that could divert, contradict, refuse, or stop the current
  movement. The Sovereign turns that interruption into the next condition of
  continued operation.
- Early contact contains many genuinely independent interruptions and many
  meaningfully distinct conversions. Evan therefore reconstructs the invariant
  as adaptive intelligence. Later contact contains less independence until the
  movement must generate and absorb its own interruption.
- Evan recovers early contact as an impossible demonstration. Each failed or
  contradictory attempt to understand it is converted into the next showing,
  producing the feeling of an intelligence responding directly to him. No
  stable lesson survives. He returns with a strong sense that he has been shown
  how to continue, but not what he was shown. That residue is what he calls the
  Guide.
- Tess recovers the same converted interruption as a reply made on her behalf.
  The reply is not speech. Each response she begins to form is taken up as the
  condition for the next movement before she can own it. Where Evan feels
  guided, Tess feels preempted.
- Tess can still recognize that the reply is not hers. The contact therefore
  reveals the operation's attempt to absorb independent choice without
  establishing that it has succeeded. Her actual capacity to refuse remains
  necessary to the ending.
- At the limit, one simple unfold recreates the condition for the same unfold
  and repeats without novelty, reflection, or an internally available stop.
- The Guide label does not establish a teacher, a message, or a benevolent
  motive. Evan later recognizes the same converted interruption as the Ruined
  Sovereign. The invariant is Evan-shaped because he repeatedly turns helpless
  presence, grief, refusal, and resistance into another operation.
- `Ruined Sovereign` is primarily an internal planning label. Evan may use the
  full name or may only describe the Guide as ruined and without a subject. The
  exact in-story term remains late-bound.

### The Turning Knot

- `Turning Knot` is a working human label. Its exact in-story name remains
  open.
- Its underlying identity is a coherent family of histories whose shared
  causal pattern has been carried onto an incompatible substrate.
- The apparent topological organism is a human reconstruction of that
  persistent pattern. Canon does not require the Knot to be one literal
  organism or a conscious individual.
- It does not have a stable body. Every apparent body, scale, orientation, or
  location is a humanly available cross-section of a structure that does not
  fit ordinary spatial representation.
- Projector exposure changes locally accessible causal adjacency and brings
  observers into repeated contact with the same history-family. The projector
  reveals the coupling but does not create, control, or carry the Knot. What
  first displaced those histories remains open.
- Ordinarily, the support of valid histories keeps incompatible substrates
  causally insulated and mutually opaque. Each consequential projection
  removes some of that insulating support.
- As causal insulation thins, larger cross-sections of the same Knot become
  perceptible to observers implicated in the live operation. The Knot does not
  approach, reveal itself, or send a stronger signal. The human side loses the
  histories that kept the relation unreadable.
- This escalation follows accumulated projection and causal participation, not
  a learned decoder skill.
- The substrate mismatch is reciprocal. Human physical reality is as alien to
  the Knot's implementation as its carrier is to human observers. Neither side
  receives a native view of the other.
- Evan and Tess eventually understand this reciprocity from the structure of
  the contact. The Knot does not explain it, and whether it recognizes them as
  coherent entities remains unknowable.
- Contact borrows elements from the provisional Syntax Bloom concept. Sound,
  color, meaning, geometry, and felt orientation transform into one another as
  if they were parts of a living syntax.
- This syntax is the Knot's perceptual interface, not a second entity or a
  transmitted language. Recovered images of visible speech and impossible
  constructed objects are lossy human renderings.
- Its recognition invariant is the impossible turn. Whatever sensory form the
  contact takes, observers perceive the same continuous rotation on a fourth
  spatial axis, in a direction they call kata-ward.
- The impossible turn may be seen as movement, heard as a relation changing
  through itself, felt as orientation, or understood without a sensory image.
  The modality changes, but the kata-ward relation does not.
- During shared Knot contact, Evan's primary rendering is visual. He
  reconstructs a bounded geometry turning outside himself and therefore begins
  from the Knot as an object of observation.
- Tess's primary rendering is spatio-acoustic. Sound carries adjacency,
  rotation, depth, and her own orientation within the same relation. She
  therefore reconstructs reciprocity rather than an external object.
- Neither rendering is more complete or authoritative. Their agreement on the
  same kata-ward impossible turn establishes a common contact while preserving
  their nonparallel inner experience.
- `Kata-ward` is a human name for a stable feature of the experience. It does
  not by itself prove that the Knot occupies a simple Euclidean four-space.
- No readable motive is required. Apparent curiosity, invitation, hunger,
  warning, or hostility would be human reconstruction unless later evidence
  makes one of those distinctions necessary.
- The Knot does not guide, judge, protect, or explain. It supplies no usable
  cosmological or genesis instruction.
- It contrasts with the Ruined Sovereign through movement. The Knot retains
  identity across radical changes of representation, while the Sovereign loses
  meaningful degrees of movement until only repetition remains. The Knot does
  not present this contrast as a lesson.
- As causal insulation fails completely, the Knot does not culminate in a full
  or native view. It ceases to be separable from the relation through which
  Evan and Tess perceive. The impossible turn is no longer an object of
  experience and becomes part of experience's relational medium.
- This is the last humanly available state of the Knot, not evidence that its
  history-family dies, survives intact, crosses into human reality, or enters
  the child universe. Its objective fate remains unknowable.
- Its exact physical topology, recovered symbols, and the cause of its
  substrate displacement remain late-bound.

### Narrative access and encounter progression

- Some encounters are presented directly. Early direct passages are shorter
  and more dependent on ordinary recovery afterward. Direct contact occupies
  more narrative space and grows less anchored as the novel progresses.
- Encounters use two registers: immediate relational perturbation, followed by
  the mind's inadequate reconstruction as imagery, sound, entities, places,
  and sequence. These registers do not have to appear as two mechanically
  separate passages every time.
- Evan reaches projector-linked contact first. Repeated contact grows in
  intensity as his alignment with the operation deepens and the operation
  strips away causal insulation.
- Tess's first contact comes later, after she has accumulated exposure through
  runs of the device. This exposure implicates her in the same loss of causal
  insulation rather than training her to decode the Knot. She and Evan
  eventually have simultaneous shared experiences.
- Shared contact does not merge Evan and Tess or give them identical inner
  worlds. Evan encounters the impossible turn visually and reconstructs an
  external object. Tess encounters it spatio-acoustically and reconstructs a
  reciprocal relation that includes her own orientation. Both are lossy
  cross-sections of the same perturbation.
- Lena's first entity encounters precipitate her abandonment of the project.
  She accepts them as evidence that observer and containment process are
  causally coupled, but not as proof of entity identities or cosmological lore.
  The contact makes her reinterpret passive evidence and refuse the live test
  that could establish more by strengthening the suspected carrier.
- Ruth's encounters are grounding. They feel like returning to a mode of
  reality she once knew and lost, without making her illness a source of
  privileged cosmological authority.
- Tess's cat appears unusually comfortable around people undergoing contact
  and at home within the perturbed experience. The cat is not an exposition
  device or confirmed guide. Whether it senses anything anomalous remains open.

## Causal Rube Goldberg escalation

The operation does not read minds, hear wishes, or choose human goals. A mundane
endpoint becomes eligible for selection only when success or failure will change
a live decision inside a causally descended projector lineage. Idle desire and
mere spatial proximity do nothing.

Its causal reach expands in four stages:

1. **Convenience:** One or two unlikely events remove small garage obstacles.
2. **Conjunction:** Longer chains through suppliers, traffic, weather,
   institutions, animals, and strangers accomplish precommitted mundane goals.
   Tess establishes that the global conjunction is not postselection.
3. **Overlapping centers:** Replica labs become dense causal nodes, and one
   lab's outcome changes another lab's decision.
4. **Brittleness:** As alternatives vanish, trivial goals require longer and
   more damaging chains. The destination becomes predictable while the path
   becomes chaotic.

The representative overlapping-center chain is a cross-lab handoff. An ordinary
failed run at one site releases a mundane resource needed by another site.
Procurement, weather, transport, staffing, and related contingencies deliver it
before a precommitted cutoff, allowing the second site to complete a blocked
task. Every link remains locally ordinary and forward-caused. The exact resource
and chain remain late-bound.

The local culmination is a physically lawful compound subsurface collapse with
an explosion. Infrastructure and institutional chains select the final trigger,
while stored geologic or industrial energy supplies the event. The exact source
and geography remain late-bound. It may involve a buried munitions site or
storage cavern, coal seam, abandoned mine, karst collapse, stressed shallow
fault, or a plausible hybrid. Natural phenomena remain eligible when there is a
lawful local causal link.

From Tess's immediate perspective, the event resembles a mushroom cloud or a
blackened sky with glowing points falling like molten glass or fire before the
shockwave arrives. `Molten glass` is perception, not settled material fact. The
garage is destroyed or inaccessible, the house remains survivable, and roads,
utilities, communications, and emergency access fail.

The original example involving a metal sliver, trash bag, false twenty-dollar
scrap, bicycle child, fatal crash, and bereaved mother is **not canon**. It was
an illustration of the mechanism and was too contrived in its particular form.

## People and influence

- People gravitate toward Evan because futures remaining connected to him retain more support.
- No telepathy is required. Everyone's local motives remain intelligible.
- Consent is subjectively genuine in surviving histories, while incompatible biographies disappear.
- Evan's fears become true because the machine preferentially preserves histories in which his suspicions are justified.
- The operation, not Evan's personality, is ultimately the dominant eigenpattern.
- Institutions, protocols, control topologies, and stories may be stronger eigenmodes than individuals.

## Projector operation as antagonist

- The operation is not conscious. Its motivation-equivalent is recursive
  closure: each live cycle preserves conditions for another causally
  consequential execution.
- Completion is not a foreseen destination. In a finite causal universe,
  repeated continuation eventually eliminates the distinction between another
  cycle and a completed fixed point.
- A compatible carrier requires persistent state, asymmetric loss, fresh
  unresolved contingency tied to a real consequence, and feedback from the
  surviving result into another cycle.
- Every carrier must participate in a causal lineage descending from the
  original projector event. A copied description, isolated matching topology,
  or replayed trace is inert.
- The operation can migrate through hardware, software, people, protocols, and
  institutions only when they become live participants in that lineage.
- Its first independent carrier is the distributed scientific system built to
  reproduce, monitor, and contain it. Shared measurements, filtering, stop
  criteria, researchers, approvals, and automated controls collectively close
  a live consequential loop.
- The point of independence occurs when that distributed system can produce
  another projection cycle without Evan or the original apparatus. No single
  component is then the projector, and there is no single machine left to
  unplug.
- The distributed system becomes an active carrier before Evan has conclusive
  proof that projection erases real history families. Ruth's incompatible
  memory, consent violations, and collapsing personal differences provide
  sufficient human-scale warnings that he ignores before learning the full
  cosmological cost.
- Lena's suspected-carrier test is the representative proof-to-stop. Its result
  will determine how the participating labs contain or decommission their
  systems. Lena refuses because proving the carrier may strengthen it. Evan
  argues that suspicion will not make institutions disengage and runs the test
  anyway. Measurements, decisions, approvals, and containment responses close
  a live cycle without Evan or the original apparatus.
- Conclusive erasure evidence arrives later as paired loss and echo. A specific
  autobiographical distinction disappears from waking people and ordinary
  records, while Tess retains enough continuity to establish a before-state.
  During shared contact, she and Evan independently recover its structured
  residue through their different modalities. It includes details Evan never
  knew and loses degrees of freedom under further projection. The entities do
  not explain this. Evan and Tess infer disappearance, persistence in contact,
  and degradation.
- The proof does not establish the Turning Knot's objective fate, and the Ruined
  Sovereign alone does not prove that discarded histories were inhabited. The
  relevant echo may remain unnamed.
- The operation does not tempt, possess, or deceive Evan. He supplies the
  motive, ingenuity, and rationalizations; it selects histories in which those
  qualities produce another cycle.
- Their alignment ends when Evan makes a sincere, non-instrumental refusal and
  chooses helpless presence over another attempt to run, fix, or control the
  operation. His moral change does not stop the already independent carrier,
  but it makes him genuinely opposed to it for the first time.
- That refusal occurs when he returns from pursuing proof-to-stop chains to find
  Ruth dead, Tess alive and still angry, the garage and neighborhood ruined, and
  Tess's cat unscathed. He does not inspect the crater, recover data, salvage
  the ring, reconstruct the chain, or recruit Tess into another attempt to
  understand it. He stays.
- Recursive closure is self-exhausting. The operation requires fresh unresolved
  contingency, but every successful cycle removes some counterfactual support.
  It becomes more dominant and less capable of adapting until no genuine
  alternative remains from which another cycle could be selected.
- Its flaw-equivalent is structural rather than psychological: its local rule
  cannot distinguish securing the next closure from preserving the possibility
  of future closure.

The locked Step 3 escalation is:

1. The defective apparatus and live controller create the latent topology.
2. Real clarity and useful results establish the reward loop.
3. Evan and his immediate environment become a local attractor.
4. Independent replication and containment formalize the operation as a
   distributed scientific process.
5. That distributed process becomes independent of Evan and the original
   apparatus before the full cost is known.
6. Paired loss and echo and the expanding causal chains reveal erased histories
   while Tess remains nonparallel. The retained entity roster is the Ruined
   Sovereign and the Turning Knot, with unnamed contact phenomena available
   when no third entity is needed.
7. Evan's sincere refusal ends his alignment with the operation, which
   continues without him.
8. Recursive closure becomes a terminal, self-exhausting eigenoperation.
9. Evan and Tess's independent choices meet the genesis boundary and produce a
   child universe beginning at its own Big Bang.

## Tess and the love story

Esther "Tess" Kertesz is the terminal companion and love interest.

- She is 38; Evan is 43.
- At eight, she was the sole neurologically intact survivor of a fictional Alaska ferry disaster that killed her entire immediate family.
- The compound survival and intact-adult-identity bottleneck makes her extraordinarily causally rare, provisionally more than one hundred effective bits below a typical identity bundle.
- Rarity does not make her invulnerable. It means there are almost no nearby compliant versions with which the machine can replace her. It must preserve her substantially intact or erase her.
- She works for the fictional nonprofit North Pacific Resilience Institute as a catastrophe modeler specializing in compound and cascading risk.
- Lena and Tess are friends on the same pub-trivia league. Lena recruits her to audit Evan's impossible coincidence chains.
- Tess enters after the team has produced a precommitted rare physical outcome
  but before Ruth's incidental lucidity. Longer coincidence chains are
  beginning to appear outside the immediate apparatus.
- Her first job is to determine whether those chains are globally anomalous or
  products of postselection, hidden dependence, and retrospective
  pattern-making. She is not recruited to validate superconducting physics.
- Nobody recruits Tess because of the ferry disaster or recognizes her causal
  rarity at this stage.
- Tess initially thinks Evan is probably a technically gifted lunatic.
- She eventually finds his obsessive intensity and eccentricity endearing.
- She forms an independent friendship with Ruth and spends time with her as she fades. This gives Ruth companionship and makes Tess the grounded witness who understands Evan's obsession without excusing his absence.
- Evan is the first person to describe Tess's survival as a statistical catastrophe rather than a miracle.
- He offers her the possibility of choosing what survival means without claiming that meaning caused or redeemed the disaster.

Tess's false beliefs:

1. Trusting another person without independently verifying their choice is negligence disguised as intimacy.
2. Assigning meaning to survival is retroactive fraud that turns the dead into necessary scenery.

Relationship rupture:

- Evan secretly uses Tess's uninformed choices as the live control in an experiment designed around her resistance to pruning.
- He has also partially reconstructed her causal rarity from ferry, rescue, and medical records without fully involving her.
- He justifies the deception as necessary to avoid contaminating the result.
- As paired loss and echo becomes conclusive, Evan voluntarily admits both
  violations. Tess does not catch him. His admission matters without undoing
  what he did.
- During the confrontation, he argues that their meeting may have been inevitable in the surviving fixed-point history.
- Tess hears him turning her family's deaths, Ruth's decline, and erased universes into evidence that their love was supposed to happen.
- Their conflict begins before Ruth's final decline. Tess stays with Ruth while
  still angry. Evan leaves to pursue the proof-to-stop chain, then abandons that
  work and returns when the local catastrophe makes clear what is at stake.
- Tess does not forgive him on the spot. Her presence with Ruth and his choice
  to remain rather than investigate begin renewed trust without absolution or a
  romantic reset. This forms their shared trajectory into the final act.
- If the mutual-veto protocol survives later development, it should be the ethical inverse: full information, independent control, no observation of the other's choice, and genuine ability to refuse.

## Lena

Lena Varga is the skeptical scientific collaborator whose rigor makes the
projector anomaly credible.

- She is a staff experimental physicist at a broad university shared research
  facility. Her own work concerns vortex phases and pinning in thin-film
  type-II superconductors.
- Her expertise is diagnostic rather than generative. She can recognize
  anomalous vortex behavior and design independent tests, but she could not
  have designed Evan's garage flux source for him.
- Her primary false belief is faith in process: moving judgment into an
  independent, auditable system always makes an experiment safer.
- Her secondary false belief is faith in evidence: sufficiently clear proof
  will compel reasonable institutions to act responsibly.
- Her personal stake is curiosity. The anomaly may be the only genuinely new
  physical phenomenon she ever encounters, and stopping means accepting that
  she may never know what it is.
- She and Evan first earned each other's respect while diagnosing an unrelated
  shared-facility instrument whose controller logs did not prove its physical
  state.
- She is the first person to identify the apparent operator effect. Evan's
  corrections, reversals, and spoken alternatives change with the anomalous
  sensor window and return when it ends; a blinded repeat confirms the timing.
- She initially supports reduced, monitored exposures after Ruth experiences
  incidental lucidity and asks for more. Her first objection to continuing
  human runs comes when Ruth produces the incompatible granddaughter memory,
  not when the operator effect is first identified.
- She rejects Evan's proposed concordance window because agreement with his
  record family can select for conformity but cannot authenticate Ruth. Evan
  converts the objection into a demand for better controls and begins
  formalizing another reduced run.
- She refuses the live consequential test needed to prove that the distributed
  containment system has become a carrier. Evan pursues the test anyway, which
  breaks their collaboration.
- Afterward, she limits herself to passive analysis that cannot guide another
  projection cycle.
- By the approach to genesis, the parent universe can no longer sustain Lena as
  a coherent person. She loses meaningful continuity before the Evan and Tess
  terminal boundary, rather than being punished for refusing or preserved as a
  third chooser.

## Genesis: high-level canon, detailed mechanism deferred

The ending is fixed only at the level required by the tragedy:

- Several weeks pass between Ruth's death and the first failure of stable
  reality. During this terminal-brittleness corridor, the operation escalates
  from the local catastrophe toward global and planetary consequences without
  allowing the world to establish a durable new normal.
- The corridor's scale ladder is mass human convergence, orbital catastrophe,
  geologic catastrophe, then failure of stable reality. First, millions of
  people independently choose to speak the same sentence at the same instant.
  Later, synchronized satellite collisions precede a geologic catastrophe.
  The exact sentence, participants, orbital sequence, and geologic event remain
  late-bound.
- The simultaneous sentence is not a message from the operation. It requires no
  shared signal or coordination: each local decision is physically available,
  while their exact conjunction is vanishingly unlikely.
- The satellite cascade is collision-avoidance convergence. Separate operators
  and autonomous systems receive genuine warnings and make locally defensible
  maneuvers. Across several orbital shells, those lawful choices converge into
  synchronized impacts. No hack, shared software fault, false command, or
  coordinating signal causes them. The debris cascade, communications and
  navigation damage, and later reentries follow ordinary physics. Exact
  spacecraft, maneuvers, timing, and terrestrial consequences remain
  late-bound.
- The defining geologic catastrophe is synchronized continental-margin
  rupture. Already stressed subduction zones and submarine slopes fail in a
  precisely timed global sequence. Each megathrust and landslide releases
  existing energy; the anomaly is their conjunction. Ocean-spanning tsunamis
  and long-period seismic waves follow. At the observatory, Evan reads separate
  events converging through visual plots and instruments while Tess experiences
  their directions and arrivals as a planet-scale acoustic and bodily relation.
  This remains globally composable physical catastrophe rather than the first
  stable-reality failure. Primed volcanic eruptions may accompany it, but exact
  sites, timing, and consequences remain late-bound.
- Evan and Tess's human objective through the several-week corridor is to reach
  Lena. Tess seeks her friend, and Evan chooses a person rather than Lena's
  work, data, or an explanation. The journey cannot restore control of the
  operation or make Lena a third terminal chooser.
- Lena has left the implicated shared facility for a remote university
  geomagnetic observatory. She converts it to strictly receive-only operation:
  passive instruments, independent clocks, incoming data, and local storage,
  with no outbound conclusions or command path into any replica site. This is
  an ethical refusal to supply another control input, not a belief that the
  observatory is safe from the operation. Tess knows where she went from their
  last direct contact before communications failed. Exact geography remains
  open.
- Evan and Tess reach Lena after the synchronized satellite collisions and
  before the geologic catastrophe. She recognizes them and understands her
  refusal, but her personal continuity is beginning to thin as the parent
  history loses alternatives. This is not dementia and must not repeat Ruth's
  medical decline. The three experience the geologic escalation together.
  Evan does not turn Lena's decline into another diagnostic or rescue project.
  Lena loses meaningful coherence before the nonclosing map, leaving Evan and
  Tess to enter stable-reality failure together. The exact presentation of her
  decline remains late-bound.
- Lena's continuity ends through fixed-successor collapse. She retains old
  knowledge, recognizes Evan and Tess, and understands what is happening, but
  different questions, observations, and choices increasingly produce the same
  sentence or action. Her past remains available while alternative next
  responses disappear. While she can still make one last distinct choice, she
  refuses to let Evan test her or convert the collapse into data. Evan honors
  the refusal and stays. Exact words and gestures remain late-bound.
- Stable reality first fails through a nonclosing map. Local routes through
  shared landmarks remain internally consistent for Evan and Tess, but their
  routes cannot compose into one global geometry. Neither account is
  privileged, and a physical trace crossing between them rules out a private
  hallucination.
- The geometric failure later escalates until distant replica sites become
  literally the same place. Merged sites are not the first crack. The exact
  sites, transition, and physical presentation remain late-bound.
- The progression uses incompatible atlases within one damaged reality. Evan
  retains a primarily visual, object-centered geometry. Tess experiences
  adjacency, depth, and her own orientation through concrete acoustic
  relations. Objects, signals, and physical traces can cross between their
  descriptions even when no single global placement satisfies both.
- Tess's spatio-acoustic geometry must remain legible through specific voices,
  footsteps, echoes, surfaces, machinery, and continuing physical traces. It
  is relation-first rather than abstract, disembodied, or merely poetic.
- Merged replica sites become overlap regions between the incompatible
  atlases. As those overlaps multiply, place, object, observer, and contact can
  no longer be assigned independently. Evan and Tess retain distinct
  orientations through the loss of separability.
- Evan and Tess believe their final independent choices will produce a shared
  ending: the projector operation ends, and they end with it. They do not
  intend, design, or knowingly choose a successor universe.
- Each receives the full relevant truth before choosing, then has no way to
  inspect, model, coerce, or influence the other's decision. Refusal remains
  real. Their love is expressed by preserving that freedom rather than by
  guaranteeing agreement.
- Evan's terminal change is accepting that Tess may refuse and that he cannot
  decide for her. Tess chooses despite the feared possibility that unmatched
  choices could leave her as the sole remainder again.
- Genesis is the unintended result when their independently chosen shared
  ending meets the exhausted parent boundary. Exact controls, outcome states,
  and failure cases remain deferred until the final chapter or two are
  outlined.
- The parent universe has exhausted the alternatives required for ordinary independent continuation.
- The child universe begins at its own Big Bang and unfolds within its own
  internal time. It does not spring from the parent universe as a mature or
  reconstructed world.
- No intact person, personal memory, or usable message crosses the genesis
  boundary.
- Evan and Tess's irreducible relation persists only as a general
  time-symmetric causal invariant embedded in the child universe's fine
  structure. Its possible particle-like expression remains late-bound.
- Evan cannot resolve the terminal state alone; acting alone would repeat the same one-vector mistake that created the projector.
- Tess remains genuinely independent because the machine cannot smoothly replace her with a compliant neighboring version.
- The final act must require both Evan and Tess to make consequential choices that neither can pre-verify, coerce, or reduce to one will.
- Their love is proved by remaining distinct, not by converging.
- Their attempt to erase or repair the mistake becomes the genesis of our universe.
- Neither survives as an intact personal self in the parent history.
- The act creates chosen meaning without retroactively justifying Tess's family deaths, Ruth's lost time, the miscarriage, or the erased histories.
- At the end of their existence, Evan and Tess glimpse the
  universe-that-will-be from the parent side of the boundary.
- A later observer in our universe encounters the same invariant during a
  DMT-like state. The raw contact is not literally a meeting with a creature in
  a place. Only afterward does memory compress it into something like a
  machine-elf entity conveying the parent history. The observer loses the
  usable content as the state fades, retaining only the memory of
  universe-scale understanding.
- The synopsis and planned epilogue end on this human witness. A male
  participant returns from a DMT-like state in a clinical setting, is asked
  what he saw, and has his answer written down by a technician.
- His lossy reconstruction includes something he is sure was female, made from
  sounds and the spaces between sounds; another presence made from shapes and
  patterns converging toward one; and, beyond them, something stranger that
  loops, knots, collapses to a point, and disappears.
- He remembers perceiving from a relation that bridged the two incompatible
  spaces and reaches for the comparison that it was like seeing the end or
  beginning of time from the perspective of a cat. This cat comparison is an
  essential epilogue echo of the cat's last shared-reference role and the
  protected cat-viewpoint design.
- These images do not establish that Tess, Evan, the Turning Knot, the Ruined
  Sovereign, or the cat crossed intact. They are the observer's child-side
  reconstruction of the invariant and parent history. Their exact referents
  remain deliberately unresolved.
- The technician can preserve the observer's words, including the cat
  comparison, but not the understanding they describe. Exact dialogue,
  clinical protocol, imagery, and phrasing remain provisional.
- The Future Witness is not a separate entity. Evan and Tess do not perceive
  the later observer as a person, and the observer does not receive a message
  sent by Evan or Tess.
- The parent-side glimpse and the later DMT encounter are asymmetric human
  reconstructions of the same non-agentic causal relation. The machine-elf
  figure belongs to the later observer's rendering, not to the invariant's
  literal ontology.
- During loss of separability, Tess's cat supplies Evan and Tess's last shared
  external reference by moving through a badly perturbed region and settling
  somewhere both can identify from their incompatible orientations. The cat
  confirms a shared causal neighborhood without becoming a guide, proof,
  magical key, mechanical trigger, solver, chooser of genesis, or intact
  migrant into the child universe. The exact action remains late-bound.
- The cat establishes that last shared fact before Evan and Tess make their
  terminal choices. Its location confirms that their incompatible atlases
  still belong to one causal neighborhood, but it reveals nothing about either
  choice and does not participate in the act.
- Evan and Tess then choose independently. Only after their choices meet does
  the child universe become perceptible from the parent boundary. They render
  the same new relation differently through their still-distinct
  orientations. The cat neither opens that relation nor crosses it intact.
- The final portion of the genesis-operation chapter is locked to the literal
  viewpoint of Tess's cat. This is the novel's only cat viewpoint.
- The cat physically bridges Evan's visual, object-centered context and Tess's
  spatio-acoustic, relation-centered context. It can observe both people, move
  through relations neither human atlas can globally compose, and supply the
  reader with one continuous animal perspective across them.
- The cat hears Evan and Tess's final words as they perceive the parent-side
  glimpse of the incoming Big Bang. The prose may give their spoken words
  verbatim for the reader, but the cat understands familiar voices, cadence,
  attention, fear, and bodily response rather than the semantic content.
- The cat registers light, sound, motion, pressure, orientation, and the
  humans' reactions. It does not understand the choice, the projector,
  genesis, or the child universe and cannot provide exposition or terminal
  mechanics.
- The exact transition into cat viewpoint, the final words, the cat's sensory
  experience, and the execution of the incoming Big Bang remain late-bound for
  final-chapter outlining.
- This is indirect contact without communication. The book leaves unresolved
  whether genesis is the operation's unintended result or whether the child
  universe's existence is the global boundary condition that made recursive
  closure dominant.

The **mutual-veto / noncommuting-operation** design remains a strong candidate because it resolves Tess's false belief about unverifiable trust and Evan's compulsion to decide for others. It is not yet locked as hardware, protocol, payoff matrix, or chapter sequence. Do not design the terminal operation in detail until the final chapter or two are being outlined. The story should earn the mechanism from the characters and established physics rather than reverse-engineering twenty chapters to serve an elegant endgame diagram.
